# 表紙の背景素材

- 用途: 冒頭の装飾用背景。本文・研究データとは別の素材。
- 生成方法: 組み込みImageGen
- 配置先: `site/corridor-cover.webp` と生成先の `docs/corridor-cover.webp`
- 元画像: 1536×1024 PNG。WebPに形式変換し、約111KBで収録。

生成プロンプト:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background illustration for the cover of a Japanese horror folklore research website, not a UI mockup. Create a cinematic wide 1536 x 1024 composition of an empty old Japanese wooden corridor at night, receding into a slightly open sliding door and an impenetrably black room. A very narrow patch of cold window light catches worn wood grain, damaged translucent shoji paper, and the threshold, enough that architectural depth and the doorway are clearly visible. Authentic restrained Japanese ghost-story mood, unsettling absence, analog film grain, charcoal and desaturated gray with a faint aged-paper tint. Place the corridor's vanishing point and black door in the right third because the website title will occupy the left third. The left third should have dark quiet negative space without foreground objects. The right half must remain visibly detailed, not crushed to solid black. No people, silhouettes, monsters, eyes, blood, gore, text, letters, logos, seals, or watermark. One continuous background image, no frames, no UI, no collages.
```
